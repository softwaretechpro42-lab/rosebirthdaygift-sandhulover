Why December 1 Is Special ❤️

DEC1

Rose, duniya ke liye 1 December bas calendar ka ek aur din hai. Mere liye yeh woh din hai jab Allah ne meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat dua is duniya mein bheji — tumhein.

Tumhare aane se pehle mujhe nahi pata tha ke kisi ka hona hi kisi ke liye itna bada tohfa ho sakta hai. Meri subahon mein umeed, meri raaton mein sukoon aur meri hansi mein wajah — sab tumhari wajah se hai.

Isi liye har saal 1 December ko main sirf tumhara birthday nahi manata, balki Allah ka shukr ada karta hoon ke is din ne mujhe tum di.

Happy Birthday, meri Rose. Tum meri mohabbat bhi ho, meri dua bhi, aur jeene ki meri sabse pyari wajah bhi. ❤️

Forever yours,
Sandhu
